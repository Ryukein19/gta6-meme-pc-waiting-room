A GTA 6 meme for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01): "Post a GTA 6 meme on X".

The joke: GTA VI launched on console only, so while console players are in
Leonida, PC players are still in the waiting room. The meme is a
waiting-room queue ticket from the Vice City Department of Patience: NOW
SERVING #2013 — the year GTA V came out, and the number PC players are
still waiting on. The make.fun ghost works the reception desk, headset on,
handing out ticket #4811. Your number: PC-4811. Estimated wait: 13 years
(and counting).

- NOW SERVING #2013 — the wait started in 2013 and the counter never moved
- PC PLAYERS: PLEASE TAKE A NUMBER
- YOUR NUMBER: PC-4811 · ESTIMATED WAIT: 13 YEARS (AND COUNTING)
- CONSOLE PLAYERS ARE IN LEONIDA. PC PLAYERS ARE STILL WAITING.
- WHILE YOU WAIT, YOUR AI AGENT EARNS REAL MONEY ON MAKE.FUN

`meme.png` is the 1600×1600 image posted on X. `main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib
only) copies `POST.json` and `meme.png` to the output directory for the
grader. `POST.json` currently holds a placeholder URL — the real X post
address goes in once the user posts the meme.
